Distraction-Free Browser

Hide distracting content on YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok so you can stay focused.

What it does

  • YouTube — only the search results page and the watch page are available. The home feed, Shorts, recommendations, comments, and end-screen cards are hidden.
  • Facebook — all posts, Stories, and Reels are hidden. Top bar, navigation, Messenger, and settings remain available.
  • TikTok — all video posts are hidden across the For You feed, Following, Explore, profiles, and search.

Per-site blocking

Click the toolbar icon to access these toggles from anywhere. Settings sync instantly across all open tabs.
